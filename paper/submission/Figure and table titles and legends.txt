Figure 1
Title: Architecture of the system.
Legend: Words pass only between the person and the frontier model. The frontier keeps no transcript and reaches its context through two tools, recall and teach. CMLM levels hand their oldest facts down as feature vectors, at no token cost, and ranked facts climb back. An exact-key integer memory (the Life) sits beside every level. A tool-recovering model can make the frontier's calls once a prequential gate allows it.

Figure 2
Title: Recall at the last turn as sessions grow, by shape.
Legend: Bars are means over 20 seeds and whiskers are 95% confidence intervals over seeds. k equals the facts per topic at the last turn, so the ceiling is 1. Hatching distinguishes the shapes without colour.

Figure 3
Title: Recall as a share of its ceiling through a 300-turn session.
Legend: Seed 1 of the run with a 3,300-token document per turn and k = 12; markers are the probe checkpoints every 50 turns. The ceiling falls as facts per topic pass k.

Figure 4
Title: Input tokens per question through the 300-turn session.
Legend: Log scale. The transcript path is counted on every turn and never sent; the CMLM path is what the frontier was actually sent. Cascade, seed 1; token counts are characters divided by four. The dashed line marks a one-million-token context window.

Figure 5
Title: Accuracy and confidence of the key head on unseen facts.
Legend: The key head was taught n keyed examples and tested on the next 30 facts of the same world, for three seeds. Lines are means and dots single seeds. Committed counts predictions whose softmax share was at least 0.6; the dashed line is the prequential gate at 0.85.
